HOW TO PLAY

  • GOAL: hop BOTBERT over every cube to flip the whole pyramid to the target colour (top-right swatch). Clear all 28 and the screen is yours.
  • HOPS ARE DIAGONAL ONLY — ↖ ↗ ↙ ↘. Arrows work as the tilted stick (↑ = up-right), or WASD / Q E Z C, or swipe the board.
  • LATER LEVELS: from level 2 a cube needs two hops (via a half-colour); from level 3 landing on a finished cube undoes it — plan the route.
  • LIFT PADS beside the pyramid carry you back to the top. Lure SNAPPER (the coil-snake) close first and it dives off after you: 500.
  • DODGE: bouncing RIVETS, SNAPPER and the amber side-climbers CLAMP & CLING. CATCH: mint COOLANT freezes the board (100), and the teal gremlins SCUFF & SMUDGE are 300 each — but they scuff your cubes back.
  • SCORING: half-colour 15, target colour 25, screen bonus 1000–5000 + 50 per unused lift. Extra bot at 8,000, then every 14,000. Three bots — falling off the pyramid costs one.
